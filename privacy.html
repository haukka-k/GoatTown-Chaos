Goatville Chaos Simulator - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

"Goatville Chaos Simulator" (the game) is made by Santeri. This policy explains what the game does with information.

Short version

The game does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no connection to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (for example trophies, unlocked characters, pretend "goat bucks", settings and best scores) in the game's own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Menu → Start over inside the game, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, files or advertising ID. The in-game "Camera", "GoatClips", "Goat Music", "Settings", "Goat Shop" and "Goat App Shop" are pretend features that exist only inside the game.

Pretend money

"Goat bucks", "Goat Pay" and the in-game shops use pretend game currency only. The game never asks for real payment details and never charges real money.

Children

The game is designed to be suitable for children. It does not ask for names, ages, e-mail addresses or any other personal information, has no chat with other people and shows no ads.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and shares no data with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
